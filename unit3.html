Gramàtica d'anglès de 2n d'ESO

Unitat 1 Obrir ➡️

there is / there are, quantificadors i pronoms objecte

Noms comptables i no comptables, articles (the, a/an), quantificadors (some, any, much, many, how much, how many) i pronoms personals objecte.

Unitat 2 Obrir ➡️

contrast de presents, verbs estàtics i tag questions

Contrast present simple vs. present continuous (rutines vs. ara mateix), verbs que no accepten -ing (stative verbs) i preguntes confirmatòries (tag questions).

Unitat 3 Activa ara

past simple, there was / there were i used to

Formes regulars (-ed) i irregulars en passat simple, regles ortogràfiques, expressions temporals (yesterday, last, ago), there was / there were i hàbits passats amb used to.

Unitat 4 Obrir ➡️

comparatius, superlatius, (not) as... as i too / enough

Adjectius comparatius (-er / more... than, less... than), el comparatiu d'igualtat (not) as... as, les estructures too / (not) enough i la forma superlativa.

Unitat 5 Obrir ➡️

verbs modals: can, could, must, should, have to i need to

Habilitat i permís amb can i could, obligació forta amb must, prohibició amb mustn't, consells amb should, i absència d'obligació amb don't have to.

Unitat 6 Obrir ➡️

past continuous i contrast amb el past simple

El passat continu (was / were + -ing, accions simultànies amb while / as) i el contrast past continuous vs. past simple amb els connectors when i while.

Unitat 7 Obrir ➡️

el futur (be going to, present continuous, will) i el primer condicional

Plans i intencions amb be going to, cites acordades amb present continuous, futur espontani amb will, i el primer condicional amb if i unless.

Unitat 8 Obrir ➡️

present perfect simple, adverbis temporals i contrast amb past simple

El present perfect simple (have/has + participi), partícules temporals (ever, never, already, just, yet, recently, so far), for / since i contrast amb el passat simple.

Unitat 3 - Bloc gramatical

Past simple, there was / there were i used to

En aquesta unitat aprendràs a parlar d'esdeveniments que van començar i van acabar en un moment precís del passat (tant amb verbs regulars en -ed com amb els verbs irregulars), a expressar l'existència d'elements en passat (there was / there were) i a descriure accions i hàbits que abans feies però que ja no fas (used to).

1 Past simple: -ed, irregulars, did / didn't
2 There was vs. there were en el passat
3 Hàbits pretèrits: used to / didn't use to
Part 1

El past simple: regulars i irregulars

Accions puntuals acabades en el passat

Verbs regulars (-ed)

Forma base + -ed

Als verbs regulars se'ls afegeix la terminació -ed en afirmativa. Cal vigilar 3 regles ortogràfiques importants:

  • - Acabats en -e: només s'afegeix -d.
    live ➡️ lived | like ➡️ liked
  • - Consonant + -y: canvia la y per -ied.
    study ➡️ studied | carry ➡️ carried
  • - 1 vocal curta + 1 consonant final: es dobla la consonant.
    stop ➡️ stopped | plan ➡️ planned
"Yesterday, they watched a film and walked home."

Verbs irregulars i fórmules

2a columna

Els verbs irregulars no segueixen cap norma fixa: cal aprendre la seva 2a columna de memòria!

go ➡️ went
see ➡️ saw
have ➡️ had
eat ➡️ ate
buy ➡️ bought
write ➡️ wrote

Negativa: subjecte + didn't + verb en infinitiu (She didn't go).

Pregunta: Did + subjecte + verb en infinitiu? (Did you see Pol?).

Expressions: yesterday, last night, two days ago, in 2021.

Part 2

There was / there were (existència en el passat)

L'equivalent en passat de there is / there are
Forma Singular / No comptable Plural comptable Exemples
Afirmativa (+) There was There were "There was a cat." / "There were three dogs."
Negativa (-) There wasn't There weren't "There wasn't any milk." / "There weren't any cars."
Pregunta (?) Was there...? Were there...? "Was there a problem?" / "Were there any questions?"
Respostes curtes Yes, there was. / No, there wasn't. — Yes, there were. / No, there weren't. Mai no es repeteix el nom, només l'auxiliar.
Part 3

L'estructura used to (hàbits passats)

"Abans solia fer..." (però ara ja no)
Afirmativa used to + verb

S'utilitza per a accions habituals o estats del passat que ja han canviat.

"I used to live in Madrid when I was young."
Negativa didn't use to

⚠️ Compte: com que didn't ja porta el passat, s'escriu use sense la lletra 'd'!

"She didn't use to like vegetables, but now she loves them."
Interrogativa Did you use to...?

A les preguntes també s'escriu use sense 'd' final a causa de l'auxiliar Did.

"Did you use to play the violin at primary school?"
Bastides i suport

L'escala de deducció de la unitat 3

1

Pas 1: cerca la pista de temps

Si veus yesterday, last night, two days ago o in 2020, la frase necessita obligatòriament el past simple.

2

Pas 2: si hi ha did / didn't, forma base!

Did i didn't ja absorbeixen el passat. Per tant, el verb principal SEMPRE torna a la forma base (infinitiu sense to).

3

Pas 3: és un hàbit del passat?

Si la frase parla d'alguna cosa que abans feies habitualment però ara ja no, utilitza used to (o didn't use to).

Detectiu d'errors freqüents a la unitat 3
❌ Error 1: verb en passat després de didn't

"She didn't went to school" ❌ (doble passat!).

✅ Correcte: "She didn't go to school".

❌ Error 2: there was amb noms plurals

"There was many people in the street" ❌.

✅ Correcte: "There were many people in the street".

❌ Error 3: oblidar treure la -d a didn't use to

"I didn't used to play tennis" ❌.

✅ Correcte: "I didn't use to play tennis".

Fitxes imprimibles d'estudi

Resum de la unitat 3 per a 2n d'ESO

Past simple

  • - Afirmativa: verb en -ed o 2a columna (visited, went).
  • - Negativa: didn't + verb base (didn't go).
  • - Pregunta: Did + subj + verb base? (Did you see?).
  • - Pistes de temps: yesterday, last, ago, in 2020.

There was / were

  • - Singular / no comptable: There was / wasn't.
  • - Plural comptable: There were / weren't.
  • - Preguntes: Was there...? / Were there...?
  • - Respostes: Yes, there was. / No, there weren't.

Used to (hàbits)

  • (+) I used to play.
  • (-) I didn't use to play.
  • (?) Did you use to play?
  • ⚠️ Recorda treure la -d darrere de didn't / did!
